Your Construction Runner app

A simple guide for operatives and anyone who uses the phone app on site.

Version 1.0  ·  September 2026

iPhone and Android  ·  Construction Runner

Support: info@construction-runner.com

This guide is for you

If you work on site, this is the app you use every day. You do not log in on the website — the Construction Runner app on your phone is where you sign in, complete induction, read RAMS and briefings, and finish your tasks.

Keep this on your phone or print it. Ask your supervisor if you get stuck.

What you need to do

First time on the app

  1. Install Construction Runner from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Sign in (or Register with your company invite code), then wait until your administrator approves you.
  3. Set Location to Always (iPhone) or Allow all the time (Android).
  4. Open Profile → My Inductions and complete induction for your site.
  5. Add an emergency contact under Profile → My Info.

Every day on site

  1. Home → Check In / Out → pick your site → stand inside the boundary → Sign In.
  2. Open Safety and acknowledge any new briefings or RAMS (Sign & acknowledge).
  3. Do your Tasks. Use Deliveries / Assets / Messages if you need them.
  4. When you leave, tap Sign Out — or walk out of the site fence and the app will sign you out.

1. Get the app and sign in

  1. Search Construction Runner on the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) and install it.
  2. Open the app. Enter your work email and password, then tap Sign In.
  3. Optional: tick Remember me. You can also turn on Face ID later in Settings.
  4. New? Tap New user? Register here. Enter Full Name, Company invite code (from your supervisor), and a password, then Register.
Waiting for approval. After you register you will see that your administrator must approve you. You cannot Sign In on site until they do. Ask your supervisor if it takes too long.
Forgot Password? On the sign-in screen tap Forgot Password?, enter your email, tap Send, then use the link in your email.

Figure 1. Sign in to Construction Runner. Register here if you are new — you still need approval before you can use the app.

2. Turn location to Always (do this once)

The app only uses location to know when you are inside the site and when you leave. It does not track you around town or keep a location history.

The first time you Check In, you will see Always location required (iPhone) or Allow all the time required (Android). Tap Continue.

PhoneWhat to tap
iPhoneFirst: Allow While Using the App. Then: Change to Always Allow. In Settings → Construction Runner → Location, it must say Always.
AndroidSettings → Permissions → Location → Allow all the time.
While Using or Allow Once is not enough. The app cannot sign you out after you leave site, so Sign In stays locked until location is Always / Allow all the time.

Figure 2. Allow Always location so you are never left signed in after you leave site.

3. The Home screen

After you are in, you land on Home. Across the bottom you have six tabs:

Home · Safety · Deliveries · Assets · Messages · Tasks

The ••• menu (top right on most screens) has Profile, Settings, Help and Sign Out.

On Home you will see if you are Signed in, Signed out, or Absent today. Tap:

  • Check In / Out — Sign in or out at your site
  • My Tasks — work assigned to you
  • Briefings — toolbox talks waiting for your signature

Figure 3. Home — start Check In / Out from here. (Illustrated layout; labels match the live app.)

4. Sign in when you arrive

  1. Home → Check In / Out.
  2. Tap the Select site pill at the top and choose today’s site.
  3. Wait until the map says Inside Site Boundary. If it says Outside, walk onto the site and tap Refresh location (in •••).
  4. If the site needs induction and you have not done it, complete Site induction then tap Complete induction.
  5. If RAMS appear, tap Accept & Sign In.
  6. Tap Sign In. Wait for “Signing you in, please wait…”
You must be inside the blue boundary to Sign In. You cannot check in from the van park if that is outside the geofence.

Figure 4. Check In / Out. Stand inside the site boundary, then Sign In.

Sign out when you leave

  • Open Check In / Out and tap Sign Out, or
  • Walk out of the site fence — you should be signed out automatically (“You left the site — signed out automatically”).

If you have no signal, Sign Out can wait and sync later. Do not stay “signed in” overnight if you have left site.

Need a day off the site? Use Mark as absent / Lock this day (this locks sign-in until midnight GMT).

5. Induction for each site

Open ••• → Profile → My Inductions. This is how you start induction on a new site (for example if you move from one job to another).

  • Not Started — tap Start Induction
  • Completed — you are good (it lasts 365 days)
  • Expired — do it again

Induction steps: Read site safety information → Acknowledge RAMS and risk assessments → Confirm you understand site rules → Complete induction.

If the site has induction required, you cannot finish Sign In until induction is Completed.

Figure 5. My Inductions — start induction here for any company site.

7. Tasks, deliveries, assets, messages

  • Tasks — work for you. Tap a task and set Open, In progress or Done. You can also open My Tasks from Home.
  • Deliveries — New delivery, add Proof of delivery and Picture of load, then Submit delivery.
  • Assets (My Assets) — kit assigned to you. View & add images, Report inspection, Close inspection.
  • Messages — conversations with your supervisors. Tap the send icon to reply.

Figure 9. Tasks — mark Open, In progress or Done.

8. Your profile

••• → Profile

  • Personal Info — name and photo. Save personal info.
  • My Info — emergency contact, medical, competency card, declarations. You can Sign In without this, but fill it in so the site can reach someone if you are hurt.
  • My Inductions — start or renew site induction.
  • User Settings — Dark Mode, Face ID, notifications, password, language, Download My Data, Sign Out.

Figure 10. Profile — My Info and My Inductions live here.

9. If you are offline

  • You need internet the first time you Sign In to the app.
  • After that, some lists still open with no signal (they may be out of date). Pull to refresh when you are back online.
  • Sign Out and near-miss reports can wait in a queue and sync later.
  • Check In still needs a live location and a connection to finish.

10. Supervisors using the app

If you are a supervisor you use the same Check In, Safety and Tasks. You also get Supervisor Tools on Home (a drawer) and you can add a task with the + button. Site lists follow the sites your Super Admin ticked for you.
