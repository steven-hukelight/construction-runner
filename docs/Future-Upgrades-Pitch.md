# Construction Runner — Future Upgrades

## Where we are today

Construction Runner already runs a site day to day: phone sign-in that knows when someone is on site (and signs them out when they leave), inductions, RAMS and briefing sign-off, near-miss reporting, tasks, deliveries, assets, messaging and subcontractor onboarding — with a web dashboard for the office.

That puts us level with the main UK competitors. The upgrades below are about moving **ahead** of them.

---

## The big idea

Most site apps are digital paperwork: they record that something was signed.

Construction Runner already knows three things paperwork apps don't:

- **Where people are** — live site sign-in and sign-out
- **What they're qualified for** — cards and certificates with expiry dates
- **What they've read** — inductions, RAMS and briefings

Every upgrade below plugs into those three, so the app doesn't just store records — **it notices when something is wrong and tells someone.**

---

## Upgrade 1 — Prove workers understood, not just signed

After reading a RAMS or toolbox talk, the worker answers 3 quick questions generated from that exact document. Different questions each time; a bad question is pulled automatically if too many people get it wrong.

**Why it matters:** a signature proves someone tapped "I agree". This proves they understood — much stronger evidence for clients, insurers and the HSE. No UK competitor combines this with live site sign-in.

## Upgrade 2 — Permits that know where, who and when

Digital permit-to-work (hot works, height, confined space, excavation, isolations), but smarter:

- Only people with **valid qualifications** can be added to a permit
- If the person who issued the permit **leaves site**, the permit is suspended automatically
- Permits are drawn on a **site map**, with warnings when two clash (e.g. hot works next to a confined space)
- **Fire-watch countdown** after hot works, with reminders and a closing photo

## Upgrade 3 — Visitors with no iPad and no blind spots

- A **QR poster** at the gate — visitors sign in on their own phone. No kiosk to buy.
- Every visitor is **linked to a host**; if the host leaves, they're reminded their visitor is still on site
- Visitors appear in the **fire roll-call** alongside workers
- Delivery drivers are **matched to their booked slot** automatically

## Upgrade 4 — CSCS cards that stay checked

- Scan a card once; the app **tracks expiry** and reminds the worker before it lapses
- Shows **who is on site today without the qualifications their job needs**
- Flags suspicious cards (same card on two people, expired card presented)

## Upgrade 5 — Works with no signal

Finish offline sign-in and sign-out so workers on sites with poor signal are still recorded accurately, and everything syncs when they reconnect.
